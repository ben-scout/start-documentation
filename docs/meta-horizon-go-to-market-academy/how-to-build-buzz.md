# How to Build Buzz: Nail Your PR Story & Strategy

Your story is everything when it comes to getting your title noticed. But delivering that story in a clear and compelling way can often be more difficult than it sounds. That’s where a strong public relations strategy comes in.

In this video, the Meta Horizon Go-to-Market Academy teams up with Just Drive Media walk you through how to craft a compelling PR strategy that builds momentum and positions your title for success.

💡 What You’ll Learn:

- Craft clear and compelling messaging
- Create press-read assets that drive awareness and coverage
- Plan and execute a PR strategy from pre-launch → launch → post-launch
- Avoid common pitfalls

# Community Building for Growth

As developers and creators, community is more than a buzzword — it’s the foundation for long-term growth. In this Go-To-Market Academy session, Infernozilla founder and CEO Natalino Carlo shares proven strategies for building, scaling, and sustaining player communities in VR and beyond. From setting up Discord for growth to running events that keep players coming back, you’ll learn practical systems that can turn fans into your most powerful growth engine.

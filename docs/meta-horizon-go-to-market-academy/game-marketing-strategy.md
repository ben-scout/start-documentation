# Game Marketing Strategy

Meta Horizon Go-to-Market Academy partners with MHCP and Start to offer launch education and resources that help developers succeed. As part of this effort, this Webinar is the first in a series covering various go-to-market strategies.

We’ve partnered with [Scout House](https://www.scouthouse.com/) to help create this series. As a content production studio that’s helped launch over 100 games, Worlds, and apps, they will guide you through developing and optimizing your marketing strategy.

In this session, you’ll learn how to:
Leverage marketing best practices to reach your target audience and launch your game, app, or world
Apply marketing and outreach strategies to diversify your audience
Identify the next steps for building your personal marketing plan

# Game Marketing Asset Creation

This session provides a clear roadmap for turning your in-world captures into the professional-quality promotional materials you need to launch or promote your world and VR game.

This session gives you the tools to:

- Define & prioritze the essential assets you need for a successful launch
- Create professional-looking key art and trailers
- Plan your content and repurpose key assets for broader reach
- Use practical templates and proven workflows to streamline production

[Access written resources and worksheets for asset creation](https://meta-horizon-creators.com/marketingassets])

# Social Media 101 for Game Dev

Meta Horizon Go-to-Market Academy, in partnership with Start, equips our community of developers and creators with expert-led education and resources for a successful launch. This ongoing series brings in industry leaders to cover the critical go-to-market strategies that help you connect with audiences everywhere.

We’ve teamed up with Unfold, the creative agency that helped build the social communities for iconic franchises like The Sims and Call of Duty. They will teach you how to apply the same strategic frameworks used by major publishers to grow and engage your player base.

Specifically, you’ll learn how to:
Adopt the ‘4Cs’ framework used by major publishers to build a data-driven social media plan.
Define an authentic brand voice that stands out in a crowded market.
Select the right social platforms for your goals and learn tactics to build a loyal community.
Track meaningful metrics like awareness, engagement, and conversion to measure your marketing ROI.

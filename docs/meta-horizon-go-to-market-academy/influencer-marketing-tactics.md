# Influencer Marketing Tactics for Games

Meta Horizon Go-to-Market Academy partners with VAL(https://www.valvr.com/) to guide you through influencer marketing strategies for your game or world.

After viewing this webinar, you’ll be able to:

- Identify content formats that will drive the most engagement for your World or VR game.
- Apply outreach strategies to engage influencers for your campaign
- Learn to measure results from your campaign

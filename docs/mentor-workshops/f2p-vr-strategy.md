# Build a Winning Free-to-Play Strategy for VR | Mentor Workshop

In this Start Mentor Workshop, Quentin (Valem) and Nils Daumann, creator of Grab, unpack the principles behind an effective Free-to-Play (F2P) strategy on Meta Quest. Discover how to make your F2P game feel like a complete and enjoyable experience, prepare monetization from day one, and scale your multiplayer community without breaking your servers. Then, follow a live Unity tutorial that shows you exactly how to integrate in-app purchases (IAPs) using the Meta SDK.

Recorded live in June 2025 as part of the Meta Horizon Start program.

💡 What you’ll learn:

- Understand the strategic reasons for choosing a Free-to-Play model
- Learn the step-by-step technical process for implementing in-app purchases (IAPs)
- Gain critical real-world lessons from a successful F2P developer

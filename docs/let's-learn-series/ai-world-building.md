# Accelerate Your World Building with AI

Step inside the new AI suite for Meta Horizon to accelerate your world building workflow. In this installation of the Start program’s Let’s Learn series, Meta Product Design Lead Yiqi Zhao walks you through how Asset, Skybox, and Code Generation AI tools can empower you to publish performant worlds faster than ever.

Recorded live in June 2025 as part of the Meta Horizon Start program.

💡 What you’ll learn:

- Practical examples of AI-assisted design and scripting
- Best practices for collaborating across platforms (VR, web, mobile)

# Bring Your Android App into Mixed Reality | Meta Spatial SDK

Meta Product Managers Alex Louie and Danny Moon reveal how Meta Horizon OS lets developers bring mobile apps directly into mixed reality with no engine switch or rebuild required. Discover how 2D Android apps are enhanced by a growing ecosystem of features like multi-screen support and theater mode, then explore how the Meta Spatial SDK lets you use Kotlin and Android Studio to build fully immersive experiences from scratch.

Recorded live in December 2024 as part of the Meta Horizon Start program.
